PILLAR 01 · Microsoft Entra

Identity Security

5 offerings

Identity is the new perimeter. Microsoft Entra secures every user, workload identity and privileged account with adaptive authentication, least-privilege access and identity governance.

Microsoft Entra ID

IAM

The foundation of identity-first security - SSO, MFA and Conditional Access enforced across every application and user. 11SI designs Entra ID architectures that eliminate password-only access and connect hybrid on-prem and cloud identities into a unified directory.

Privileged Identity Management

PIM

Administrative accounts are the highest-value target in any environment. PIM enforces just-in-time, time-bound privileged access - ensuring elevated roles are never permanently assigned, with full audit trails for every use.

Identity Governance

IGA

Access that accumulates unchecked creates privilege creep and compliance exposure. 11SI implements Entra Identity Governance with automated access reviews, lifecycle workflows and entitlement management - ensuring the right people have the right access at all times.

Passwordless Authentication

PWL

Passwords are the leading cause of identity breach. 11SI deploys FIDO2 security keys, Windows Hello for Business and Microsoft Authenticator passkeys - eliminating credentials as an attack vector and improving the user experience simultaneously.

Conditional Access

CA

Conditional Access is the Zero Trust policy engine for identity. 11SI designs context-aware policies that evaluate user risk, device compliance and location - granting, blocking or step-up authenticating every session based on real-time risk signals.

PILLAR 02 · Microsoft Defender + Intune

Endpoint Security

4 offerings

Unified endpoint protection combining prevention, detection, vulnerability management, compliance and device management.

Microsoft Defender for Endpoint

MDE

Enterprise-grade EDR and XDR providing real-time threat detection, automated investigation and response across every endpoint. 11SI deploys and tunes MDE with custom detection rules, attack surface reduction policies and threat hunting workflows tailored to your environment.

Microsoft Intune

MDM

Cloud-native unified endpoint management for Windows, macOS, iOS and Android. 11SI implements Intune with compliance policies, configuration profiles and app management - ensuring every device meets security baselines before accessing corporate resources.

Endpoint Privilege Management

EPM

Local admin rights on endpoints are a critical attack vector. EPM removes standing local admin while allowing controlled application elevation through policy - enforcing least-privilege without disrupting user productivity or requiring IT helpdesk involvement.

Device Compliance

DCM

Non-compliant devices are blocked from accessing corporate resources through Conditional Access integration. 11SI builds compliance policies covering OS patch levels, disk encryption, AV health and Defender status - with automated remediation workflows to close gaps before they become risks.

PILLAR 03 · Microsoft Defender for Office 365

Email & Collaboration Security

4 offerings

Protects Exchange, Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive from phishing, malware, business email compromise and data leakage.

Defender for Office 365

MDO

Multi-layer email threat protection covering phishing, BEC, malware, Safe Links and Safe Attachments. 11SI deploys MDO with attack simulation training, anti-impersonation policies and advanced phishing detection - closing the gap that Exchange Online Protection alone cannot cover.

Microsoft Teams Security

TEAMS

External collaboration in Teams introduces phishing, malware sharing and data leakage risks. 11SI configures Teams governance - external access policies, guest controls, safe file scanning and communication compliance - to secure collaboration without blocking business.

SharePoint & OneDrive Security

SPO

Sensitive files shared externally or stored without governance create compliance and leakage exposure. 11SI integrates SharePoint and OneDrive with Purview sensitivity labels, DLP policies and conditional access - ensuring files are protected regardless of where they travel.

Exchange Online Protection

EOP

The foundational email hygiene layer - spam filtering, malware detection, connection filtering and threat intelligence applied to every inbound and outbound message. 11SI configures EOP with hardened policies, DMARC/DKIM/SPF enforcement and advanced anti-spam rules.

PILLAR 04 · Microsoft Defender for Cloud

Cloud Security

4 offerings

Unified cloud posture management and workload protection across Azure, AWS and Google Cloud.

Microsoft Defender for Cloud

CSPM

Continuous cloud security posture management and workload protection across Azure, AWS and GCP. 11SI deploys Defender for Cloud with custom security initiatives, Secure Score improvement programmes and automated remediation workflows - closing misconfigurations before they become incidents.

Defender for Cloud Apps

PILLAR 06 · Microsoft Purview

Data Security & Compliance

5 offerings

Discover, classify, protect and govern sensitive data across Microsoft 365, cloud platforms and on-premises repositories.

Microsoft Purview

GOV

A unified data governance platform that maps, classifies and governs sensitive data across cloud, on-premises and SaaS sources. 11SI implements Purview data estate discovery, sensitivity label taxonomies and governance policies aligned to GDPR, DPDP, SEBI and industry-specific regulatory requirements.

Data Loss Prevention

DLP

Sensitive data leakage through email, Teams, SharePoint and endpoints is prevented through contextual DLP policies. 11SI designs and deploys Purview DLP with custom sensitive information types, adaptive protection and endpoint DLP coverage - enforcing data controls without disrupting business workflows.

Information Protection

MIP

Sensitivity labels classify and protect documents and emails based on their content and context - applying encryption, watermarking and access restrictions that travel with the file regardless of destination. 11SI builds label taxonomies and auto-classification policies aligned to data governance requirements.

Insider Risk Management

IRM

Behavioural signals across Microsoft 365 - unusual download patterns, communication anomalies, departing employee activity - are correlated into risk scores that surface early before a data incident occurs. 11SI implements IRM with privacy-preserving investigation workflows and integration with HR and legal processes.

eDiscovery & Compliance

EDC

Legal hold, content search and audit trail capabilities spanning Exchange, Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive. 11SI configures Purview eDiscovery with custodian management, review sets and export workflows - enabling legal and compliance teams to meet regulatory requests reliably and defensibly.

FAQ

Common questions from
security and IT leadership.

What Microsoft Security solutions does 11SI implement?

11SI implements the full Microsoft Security portfolio - Microsoft Entra ID, Privileged Identity Management, Conditional Access, Microsoft Defender for Endpoint, Defender for Office 365, Defender for Cloud, Defender for Cloud Apps, Defender XDR, Microsoft Sentinel, Security Copilot, Microsoft Intune, Microsoft Purview and Azure Security. We deliver each as part of an integrated architecture rather than isolated product deployments.

How does Microsoft Defender XDR improve security operations?

Defender XDR correlates signals from endpoints, identities, email and cloud apps into unified incidents - reducing alert noise and enabling automated investigation. SOC analysts see correlated attack chains rather than isolated alerts, cutting mean time to respond from hours to minutes with AI-assisted investigation and automated containment actions.

What is Microsoft Sentinel and how does 11SI deploy it?

Microsoft Sentinel is a cloud-native SIEM and SOAR platform. 11SI deploys Sentinel with custom analytics rules, KQL detection queries, data connectors across the Microsoft and third-party estate, automation playbooks for common response actions, and threat hunting workbooks - delivering AI-powered security operations at cloud scale and cost.

How does Microsoft Entra support Zero Trust?

Microsoft Entra is the identity foundation of Zero Trust. It verifies every access request through Conditional Access policies that evaluate user identity, device health, location and real-time risk signals - granting least-privilege access and blocking risky sessions. Combined with PIM and Identity Governance, it ensures no implicit trust exists in your environment.

What is Microsoft Purview used for?

Microsoft Purview is a unified data governance and compliance platform. It discovers, classifies and protects sensitive data across Microsoft 365, Azure and on-premises sources using sensitivity labels, DLP policies, insider risk management and eDiscovery - helping organisations meet GDPR, DPDP, SEBI and industry-specific compliance requirements.

Can 11SI migrate us from our existing SIEM or EDR platforms?

Yes. 11SI has extensive experience migrating from legacy SIEM platforms (Splunk, QRadar, ArcSight) to Microsoft Sentinel and from third-party EDR (CrowdStrike, Carbon Black, SentinelOne) to Microsoft Defender for Endpoint. We run parallel operations during migration to ensure zero coverage gaps, with custom detection rule translation and validation.

Does Microsoft Security support hybrid and multi-cloud environments?

Yes. Microsoft Defender for Cloud provides CSPM and workload protection across Azure, AWS and GCP. Sentinel ingests signals from multi-cloud and on-premises sources. Entra ID manages identities across hybrid environments. 11SI builds unified governance that provides consistent security posture regardless of where workloads run.

How long does a Microsoft Security implementation typically take?

Individual workload implementations (Entra ID, Defender for Endpoint, Sentinel) typically take 5-15 days for standard scope (up to 500 users/devices). A full Microsoft Security platform deployment - identity, endpoint, email, cloud, SOC and data protection - takes approximately 45 days. Larger enterprises and multi-geography deployments are scoped at the assessment phase.

Does 11SI provide managed Microsoft Security services after deployment?

Yes. 11SI provides 24x7 managed security services covering continuous monitoring, threat detection and response, platform administration, policy optimization, compliance reporting and ongoing Secure Score improvement. Our managed services operate under defined SLAs to ensure your Microsoft Security platform stays at peak effectiveness.

Can Microsoft Security integrate with our existing third-party tools?

Yes. Microsoft Sentinel supports 300+ data connectors for third-party security tools, cloud platforms and SaaS applications. Defender XDR integrates with existing ITSM, SOAR and ticketing systems. 11SI builds integration architectures that connect Microsoft Security with your existing tooling - ensuring no signal is lost during or after migration.

How does Security Copilot improve SOC operations?

Security Copilot is an AI assistant embedded across Microsoft Security. It summarises incidents in natural language, generates investigation steps, explains KQL queries, identifies root causes and recommends response actions. 11SI integrates Copilot into SOC workflows so analysts handle incidents faster with greater accuracy and lower cognitive load.

What Microsoft licensing model should enterprises consider?

Microsoft Security licensing depends on your environment size, required capabilities and existing Microsoft 365 entitlements. The most common approach is Microsoft 365 E5 Security (which bundles Defender, Entra P2 and Purview), with Sentinel and Defender for Cloud billed on consumption. 11SI assesses your current licensing and recommends the most cost-effective model.

Can 11SI assess our current Microsoft security posture?

Yes. 11SI's Microsoft Security Readiness Assessment covers your current Secure Score, identity security posture, endpoint protection maturity, cloud configuration, data governance controls and SOC capability - producing a prioritised improvement roadmap mapped to your business objectives and licensing investments. It is typically the starting point for all engagements.
