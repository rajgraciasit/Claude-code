MODERN CHALLENGES

Identity is now the primary
attack surface. And the primary perimeter.

80%+ of breaches involve compromised credentials. The enterprise perimeter has dissolved - identity is what stands between attackers and your most sensitive systems.

Credential theft & phishing

Adversaries target usernames and passwords because they are still the primary authentication mechanism for most enterprises - social engineering and phishing campaigns now succeed in minutes, not hours.

Identity fragmentation

Employees, contractors, partners and customers managed in separate identity systems - AD, LDAP, HR platforms and cloud directories - create inconsistent policies, orphaned accounts and access sprawl that attackers exploit.

SaaS sprawl

Hundreds of SaaS applications each with separate credentials, separate MFA and separate access management - no central visibility, no consistent enforcement and a helpdesk drowning in password resets.

Privilege abuse

Over-permissioned accounts, standing privileged access and unmonitored admin sessions create pathways that attackers escalate into full enterprise compromise once initial access is obtained through a single credential.

Multi-cloud identity gaps

Cloud workloads, APIs and services introduce non-human identities - service accounts, tokens and machine credentials - that proliferate outside the governance processes designed for human users.

Compliance & audit failures

Access certifications, Joiner-Mover-Leaver controls and entitlement evidence required by PCI DSS, ISO 27001, SOX and DPDP cannot be sustained through manual quarterly processes at enterprise scale.

Third-party identity risk

Vendors, contractors and partner organisations access corporate systems with credentials managed outside your governance perimeter - creating exposure that most IAM programmes do not adequately address.

AI-driven attacks

AI-generated phishing emails, deepfake authentication bypass attempts and automated credential-stuffing attacks accelerate the speed and scale at which identity-based threats are executed against enterprise environments.

The Okta Identity Security Platform is a cloud-native identity platform that unifies Workforce Identity, Customer Identity (CIAM), Universal Directory, Identity Governance, Lifecycle Management, Privileged Access, Identity Threat Protection and Zero Trust capabilities into a single identity layer. It secures employees, partners, customers, contractors and machine identities across applications, APIs and devices using centralized identity policies.

Workforce Identity is Okta's solution for securing employee, contractor and partner access to enterprise applications. It includes Single Sign-On, Adaptive Multi-Factor Authentication, Universal Directory, Lifecycle Management and Device Trust - providing a single identity layer for every workforce user across cloud and on-premises applications.

Customer Identity (CIAM) is Okta's solution for securing customer-facing digital experiences. It provides branded registration, social login, progressive profiling, passwordless authentication, API access management and bot protection - enabling secure, frictionless onboarding for web and mobile applications at scale.

Identity Governance and Administration (IGA) automates the management of who has access to what. Okta IGA includes access certifications, entitlement management, access request workflows, Separation of Duties policies and compliance reporting - enforcing least-privilege access continuously rather than through manual periodic reviews.

Universal Directory is Okta's cloud-based identity store that consolidates users from Active Directory, LDAP, HR systems and external directories into a single, governed profile for every user. It provides real-time synchronisation, profile management and group-based access policies across the enterprise.

Lifecycle Management automates the provisioning and deprovisioning of user accounts across all connected applications. HR-triggered Joiner-Mover-Leaver workflows ensure new employees get Day-1 access, role changes update entitlements immediately and leavers are deprovisioned within minutes of termination.

Okta supports Zero Trust by continuously verifying identity at every access request - not just at login. Adaptive MFA, device trust, context-aware access policies, Identity Threat Protection and risk-based authentication ensure that trust is never implicitly granted and every session is evaluated against real-time risk signals.
